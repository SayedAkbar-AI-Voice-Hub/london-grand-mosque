# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Four overlapping audiences: local Muslim residents of Ellesmere Port who visit regularly for prayer times, events, and services; donors and sponsors (both Muslim and non-Muslim) who fund operations and expansion; non-Muslim neighbours and community organisations seeking interfaith connection or facility use; and new Muslims or visitors exploring the mosque for the first time. Primary surface is the public-facing website — no authenticated portal.

## Product Purpose

The Ellesmere Port Mosque website is the community's front door — a place to check prayer times, discover events and classes, learn about the mosque's leadership and mission, make donations, and make contact. Success means anyone, regardless of background, can arrive on the site and immediately feel welcomed, informed, and moved to engage.

## Brand Personality

Rooted, welcoming, dignified. The mosque has decades of presence in Ellesmere Port; the site should feel established and trustworthy, never flashy. Warm enough that a non-Muslim neighbour doesn't feel like a stranger. Grounded enough that a lifelong member feels proud to share it.

## Anti-references

- Generic charity/NGO sites that look like templates (stock hero image + three icon cards + a donate button)
- Overly corporate or cold institutional sites
- Busy, ornate "traditional Islamic decorative" sites that prioritise pattern over usability
- Sites that feel like they are trying to prove something to outsiders rather than serve their community

## Design Principles

1. **The community comes first.** Prayer times and upcoming events are never buried — the most-used information is the most visible.
2. **Dignity over decoration.** Every visual choice should make the space feel respected, not dressed up.
3. **One door for everyone.** Whether a regular worshipper, a first-time visitor, or a potential donor, no one should feel like they're in the wrong place.
4. **Show, don't explain.** The mosque's warmth and purpose should be felt through imagery, copy, and layout — not stated in a mission-statement paragraph.
5. **Earned trust.** Charity registration, transparent donation allocation, and accessible contact details should be easy to find — trust is built in details.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA minimum. Considerate of older community members (larger touch targets, clear contrast). Reduced motion support for animations. All content readable without JavaScript for users on slow connections.
